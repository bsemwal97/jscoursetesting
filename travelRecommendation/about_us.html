HELLO, WE’RE ROAM

A starting point.
A fresh perspective.

Roam is a travel recommendation concept that helps curious explorers discover beaches, temples and cities around the world. Our goal is simple: make finding your next destination feel inspiring and easy.

Browse a small collection of places, learn what makes each one special, and let your curiosity choose the direction. This educational project demonstrates HTML, CSS, JavaScript and fetching local JSON data.
